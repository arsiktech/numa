Delete your Numa Pro account

Updated 13 November 2026

In the app

  1. Sign in to Numa Pro on your phone or at numaworth.com.
  2. Open Settings, then ACCOUNT, and choose Delete account.
  3. If you want your records, choose Download a copy first.
  4. Type delete to confirm, then choose Delete my account.

Seven days to change your mind

Deletion is scheduled seven days after you ask. During that time you can sign in and choose Keep my account. AI replies are paused while deletion is pending. After the seven days, erasure starts and can no longer be cancelled. Large accounts are erased in several batches.

What is erased

  • Your account, sign-in methods and sessions.
  • Every space you own, with all its records: transactions, assets, debts, holdings, wallets, goals, limits, history and imports.
  • What belongs to your account: preferences, AI sharing choices, connected agents and their activity, phone and browser alert registrations, feedback and active-day records.

What is kept

  • Spaces someone else owns keep their records. Your membership is removed; descriptions or notes in them may still name you. Email us about those.
  • Files you or others exported, notifications already delivered, and provider logs and backups, which follow each provider's own retention.
  • Support emails, which we assess one by one.

Deleting your Numa Pro account does not delete your Apple or Google account. If you used Sign in with Apple, Numa Pro tries to revoke its saved Apple token when erasure starts. The privacy policy has the full details.

Without the app

If you cannot sign in, email support@numaworth.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.
