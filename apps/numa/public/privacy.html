Ask and third-party AI

Manual entries and some answers are handled locally. Before an AI request, Numa Pro asks whether you want to share information with Anthropic, which provides Claude. The server checks your current sharing choice, account and deletion state before authorizing the request.

An authorized request can include your question, recent conversation and a summary of your selected space. That summary can contain financial names, amounts, balances, debts, due dates, holdings, costs, flow/category labels and net-worth history. The generated summary leaves out your account email and record identifiers, but information you type can contain personal details.

Choose Not now to keep the question unsent. Allow and send confirms the choice and sends the question. You can turn AI sharing off in Settings. Withdrawal stops new authorizations; requests already authorized or underway may finish. A changed disclosure can require a new choice. AI replies are paused while account deletion is pending.

AI can suggest financial changes, but nothing is saved merely because an AI request was sent. Review a draft and press Save to save it. Saving a draft is separate from permission to send information to Anthropic.

Anthropic describes standard API input/output deletion within 30 days, with exceptions for different agreements, some services, policy enforcement and legal requirements. Flagged material can have longer retention. Numa Pro does not promise zero retention or EU-only AI processing. See Anthropic's API retention information.

Services and recipients

  • Convex hosts accounts, financial records, selected imports, connection records, the database and backend.
  • Cloudflare serves the website and handles connection/security information. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards support correspondence.
  • Apple provides sign-in identity when you use Apple sign-in, native notification delivery where enabled, and the iCloud mailbox used for support correspondence.
  • Google provides sign-in identity when you use Google sign-in. For a linked Google Sheet, a document/tab request goes to Google and the exported sheet passes through Convex for preview. This is not general Google Sheets account access.
  • Resend receives recipient addresses, verification/reset codes and message text to deliver account emails.
  • Anthropic receives authorized Ask input, recent conversation and the financial summary described above.
  • Expo and notification platforms process update-related connection/app information and, when enabled, phone push tokens, alert content and navigation identifiers. Alerts can contain debt or category names.
  • Frankfurter receives currency requests from the device and ordinary connection metadata, not your account balance.
  • Finnhub, CoinGecko, Coinbase and Yahoo Finance receive backend requests for market data, such as ticker/product identifiers and date ranges.
  • Wallet and DeFi services can receive public addresses or wallet-linked queries, depending on the network and feature. These include mempool.space, Blockscout/Optimism explorers, Alchemy, PublicNode, Morpho and Fluid. DefiLlama receives identifiers used for pricing. Public blockchain information can identify people.
  • Services and agents you connect receive information within their granted scope. A connected portal can receive scheduled backend requests to its supplied URL; Convex stores that URL and any supplied bearer credential needed for the connection. Disconnecting prevents further authorized access through that connection, but does not automatically erase copies already obtained.

A local CSV or Excel file is parsed in the app before confirmation. A linked Google Sheet is downloaded through the backend. Confirmed records are saved to your selected space. A provider's inclusion here is not an endorsement or a guarantee about its independent processing.

Retention, export and account deletion

Core account and financial records remain while you keep your account open. Account creation does not set an automatic expiry for those records. Settings provides available export and deletion controls. The in-app export covers the selected space; contact us for a request that covers other personal information.

For ordinary accounts, deletion is scheduled after a seven-day cancellation period. You can choose Keep my account during that period. Once destructive deletion starts, cancellation is no longer available. Large accounts can take several bounded batches to erase.

Deletion removes the account, owned spaces and their records, supported user-specific information and owned authentication/session records. It does not delete spaces owned by someone else. Their shared descriptions, notes, card labels or references may still identify a person even after a membership label becomes Former member. Contact us about those records. Shared ownership does not automatically override an erasure request or remove your privacy rights.

  • Newly stamped abuse counters become eligible for removal after their enforcement window plus 24 hours. Legacy unstamped and some shared authentication counters do not follow that rule.
  • Agent activity becomes eligible for removal after 90 days. The daily cleaner is bounded, so this is not a guaranteed completion deadline under backlog. Account deletion separately drains the user's activity.
  • Some older attachments and diagnostics need an ownership assessment before removal. We do not claim they have all been erased.
  • Provider logs, backups, delivered notifications and files exported by you or others are not all removed by the Numa database cascade. Backup and provider retention follow the relevant service and legal requirements.
  • Resend documents standard 30-day email/log retention, enterprise variations and seven-day backups. Its customer-account termination period is not a deadline for each Numa user's deletion.
  • Deleting a Numa account is not deletion of your Apple, Google or other external account. When a usable Apple authorization is retained, deletion processing attempts revocation. Older connections may need the manual steps shown in the app. Provider success is not a condition for starting local account deletion.

Support correspondence is stored in the support mailbox. There is currently no automatic expiry. We assess erasure requests and any necessary retention for legal obligations or claims individually. We do not promise a 90-day automatic support-mail purge.
