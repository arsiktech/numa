From inside the app

When you are signed in, open Settings, then FEEDBACK, and choose Send feedback. Your message is sent with your account and the app version. It is deleted when you delete your account.

What to include

  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • Your device, and the app version shown at the bottom of Settings.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Turn on Hide amounts in Settings first if you do not want to share figures.

Never send passwords, sign-in codes, wallet private keys, seed phrases or full financial exports.

Your account and records

  • Export: open Settings, then DATA, and choose Download a copy. It saves the selected space as one file.
  • Delete your account: open Settings, then ACCOUNT, and choose Delete account. See how account deletion works.
  • Privacy requests: for access, correction, export or deletion requests, email us. The privacy policy explains what we keep and why.

What Numa Pro does not do

Numa Pro is a record-keeping tool. It does not hold funds, move money or connect to your bank, so it cannot send, stop or reverse a payment. For those, contact your bank, exchange or card issuer.
